## Paragraph bio

I was ordained in 1982 and received degrees from The Colorado College, Nashotah House, and Notre Dame. From my 20’s I had felt called to do ministry with the poor and outcast and hoped I could support such ministry through teaching, in high schools, colleges, and universities.  For over 40 years I have been blessed to basically live out that hope for my ministry. I retired from parish ministry in 2012 in order to teach at Chaminade University. Mary Pat and I then moved to the mainland from Hawaii in 2014 in order to be closer to family. We have 7 children, living from coast to coast across the continent.
