Model Variations

Traditional Filters:  
Models like the KDTE204KPSS use a removable triple-filter system that requires manual cleaning every 1–2 months.

True Filtration:  
Newer models (e.g., KDTM404ESS3, KDTM604KPS) feature a maintenance-free system with a foreign object cup or self-cleaning mechanism that does not require regular filter removal. 

Older Models:  
Some older units use a hard food disposer or a non-removable coarse screen that should be wiped clean in place.
